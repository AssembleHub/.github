Hey! We’re Assemble, the organization behind the Assemble Hub, and possibly other things in the future!

Our platform allows anyone to advertise and showcase their projects or events and for communities to raise money and find passionate volunteers. Freelancers or businesses can also use Assemble to advertise, collect reviews, and easily communicate with clients! 

Assemble **runs entirely on donations, sponsorships, and affiliate revenue!** We've got large plans but limited capital. Please (consider donating)[https://hcb.hackclub.com/donations/start/assemble-org]!
*Assemble currently has just started development and isn't a full platform yet.*
